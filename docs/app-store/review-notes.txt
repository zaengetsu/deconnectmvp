2. PURPOSE AND AUDIENCE
Rekonect helps families reduce children's screen time in a positive way. Parents (adults) create an account and propose offline activities to their children (cooking, sport, reading, creativity, helping out). The child completes the activity, sends a short note or photo as proof, the parent validates it and the child earns points that unlock rewards chosen by the parents. Target audience: parents of children aged about 6 to 14. The parent account is always the account holder; a child can only use the app through a profile created by the parent and protected by a PIN.

3. HOW TO ACCESS THE MAIN FEATURES
Demo parent account: credentials are in the Sign-In Information fields (one child, "Leo", already linked; child PIN: 1234).
- Parent space: home dashboard, children tab, "Validations" tab (one activity is waiting to be validated), "Récompenses" (rewards) tab, the "+" button to assign activities from the catalogue.
- Child space on the same device: "Réglages" (avatar at top right) > "Basculer vers l'espace enfant" > select Leo > PIN 1234. The child sees assigned challenges, points, levels and rewards. To go back, log out and log in again with the parent account.
- Account deletion: "Réglages" > "Mon compte" > "Supprimer mon compte", confirmed with the password. It permanently deletes the family's data.
- Privacy policy and support: "Réglages" > "Confidentialité" and "Aide et support" (also linked on the sign-up screen).
No purchase is possible in the iOS app: the "Mon abonnement" screen only displays the current plan and its limits.

User-generated content: the child's notes and photos are private to the family and are only shown to the child's own parents; nothing is public and users cannot contact strangers. Parents can report any activity from the "Validations" screen ("Signaler cette activité"); reports are reviewed by the Rekonect team in our back office. Partner offers shown to parents are moderated by our team before publication and are never shown to children.

4. EXTERNAL SERVICES
- Scaleway (France): hosting of our API and back office.
- Supabase (EU): managed PostgreSQL database.
- Apple Push Notification service: notifications.
- Brevo: transactional emails (password reset, confirmations).
- Stripe: payments for business partners on our website only; not used in the iOS app.
No AI service, no advertising SDK, no third-party analytics or tracking.

5. REGIONAL DIFFERENCES
The app is currently distributed in France and its interface is in French (on-screen labels are quoted in French above). Features and content are identical for all users.

6. REGULATED INDUSTRY / THIRD-PARTY MATERIAL
Rekonect does not operate in a regulated industry and does not include protected third-party material. Children's data is processed under the GDPR with the parent as account holder; there is no advertising aimed at children. The app is not listed in the Kids category.
